LoopStop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

LoopStop is a screen-time tool that interrupts endless scrolling in Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. This policy describes what the app does with data. The short version: nothing leaves your phone, because nothing is collected.

What LoopStop accesses

LoopStop uses Android's Accessibility Service to read the visible on-screen UI text of three apps only — Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube — while one of them is open. It uses this to recognize when a feed has turned into an endless scroll (for example, when one video is replaced by the next) so it can step in.

What LoopStop does NOT do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app requests no network permission at all, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything.
  • It does not store or transmit what it sees. Visible UI text (which can include a post's visible caption) is only matched in memory to recognize that the on-screen video changed, then discarded immediately; it is never written to disk, logged, or retained.
  • It does not read or handle passwords, private messages, photos, videos, or screenshots.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What is stored on your device

LoopStop keeps a small amount of local data on your phone only:

  • Your settings (which locks and exceptions you have toggled on or off).
  • A daily counter of blocked scroll attempts and the label and time of the most recent one (for example, "Stopped a Reels scroll at 3:12 PM"). This never includes the content that was on screen.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Accessibility Service disclosure

LoopStop's use of the Accessibility Service exists solely to detect scroll continuation in the three supported apps and interrupt it, as described above and disclosed in the app before the permission is requested. It is not used for any other purpose.

Children

LoopStop does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date. Since the app cannot collect data by construction, changes would only ever be editorial.
